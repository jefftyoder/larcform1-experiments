Do not use em-dashes, double-hyphens as em-dash substitutes, or single hyphens functioning as dashes in any writing. Use standard punctuation instead: commas, colons, semicolons, periods.

**Why:** User explicitly requested this for abstract writing and finds dash substitutes objectionable, not just em-dashes themselves.

**How to apply:** When drafting or editing prose for Jeff, never reach for an em-dash or any stand-in (` -- `, ` - `). Restructure with commas, colons, semicolons, or split into separate sentences.
